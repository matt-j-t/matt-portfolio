About

A bit about me

A passion for drawing, designing and modelling from a young age has carried through into my professional life. Creatively solving problems and using design thinking to drive ideas through to implementation remains a key motivator in everything I do.

Throughout my career I've developed a wide variety of skills — from sketching and CAD to XR and storytelling — enabling me to work as a versatile designer and problem solver. In my most recent roles I've also grown as a leader, managing high-profile projects and cross-functional teams while staying hands-on with the craft.

Outside of work, I'm a keen traveller who's always exploring somewhere new — from the hidden corners of Birmingham to the golden triangle. I was previously a committed member of Team GB's fencing squad, and I still draw a lot of creative inspiration from design shows and exhibitions around the world.
